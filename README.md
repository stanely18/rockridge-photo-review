# Rockridge photo review

A small static site for the CY290 sidewalk-audit team (Team SCCRM). Each teammate picks their own name and reviews the photos they took in Rockridge on the 2026-11-11 field audit.

- **Required:** photos that Stanley (with AI help) judged to show a clear sidewalk problem. Do you agree?
- **Optional:** all the other photos. Mark any you think show a problem.
- Answers are saved in your browser only. Press **Download my review** and send the CSV to Stanley.

A sidewalk problem means a sidewalk that is *clearly* problematic for people with limited mobility: blocked or too narrow, discontinuous, broken or uneven pavement, or a missing or unusable curb ramp.

Photos are 1200 px copies with EXIF removed. The coordinates and street segments of each photo are public information about public streets.
