# Sidewalk audit photo review

A small static site for the CY290 sidewalk-audit team (Team SCCRM). Pick the audit (Rockridge, 2026-11-11; Chinatown audit 1, 2026-10-09; or Chinatown audit 2, 2026-10-28), then your own name, and review the photos you took.

- **Required:** photos flagged as likely sidewalk problems. Do you agree? In Rockridge and Chinatown audit 1 these are Stanley's reviewed labels; in Chinatown audit 2 they are an **AI pre-screen that no person has checked yet**.
- **Optional:** all the other photos. Mark any you think show a problem.
- Answers are saved in your browser **and sent to the team Google Sheet** a few seconds after each rating. **Download my review** gives you a backup CSV. Use **Test mode** first to check that uploading works.

A sidewalk problem means a sidewalk that is *clearly* problematic for people with limited mobility: blocked or too narrow, discontinuous, broken or uneven pavement, or a missing or unusable curb ramp.

Photos are 1200 px copies with EXIF removed. The coordinates and street segments of each photo are public information about public streets.
